# Gallery Media Plan

Status: planning. No gallery assets have been selected yet.

Follow the [gallery product brief](../docs/product/pages/gallery.md): curate a
broader visual survey of the work rather than duplicating the case-study index.

## Initial Direction

Start with the case-study captures, then select images that are interesting
outside the thumbnail crop: a larger toolbar panel, an embedded application
state, or a legible technical artifact. Preserve originals in their project
folders and reference them here rather than duplicating files.

The gallery can use different aspect ratios from the carousel. Choose its crops
after seeing the available captures; a good thumbnail is not automatically a
good gallery image.

## Selection

No selections yet. For each chosen item, record its project spec, original file,
proposed crop, caption, alt text, related case study, and intended position.

## Open Decisions

- Should recordings appear in the gallery? The current product brief specifies
  images; update it when a recording direction is chosen.
- If clips are included, should they play inline or after selection, and what
  still poster should represent each one?
- Which captures broaden the collection beyond the five carousel covers?
