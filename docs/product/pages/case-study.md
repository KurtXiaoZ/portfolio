# Case-Study Page

## Purpose

Demonstrate the quality of Kurt's work through a clear account of the problem, his contribution, the decisions he made, and the resulting impact.

## Suggested Content Structure

1. Project summary
2. Context and problem
3. Kurt's role and responsibilities
4. Constraints and challenges
5. Approach and important decisions
6. Execution and collaboration
7. Outcomes and impact
8. Reflection and lessons learned

The structure may be adapted when a project is better explained in a different sequence.

## Content Requirements

- Make Kurt's personal contribution distinct from the team's work.
- Explain the reasoning behind important decisions, not only the final result.
- Use verified facts and metrics; do not invent missing details.
- Include only information that is appropriate for a public portfolio.
- Generalize or omit confidential employer, customer, or system information.

## Navigation

Each case study should provide a clear way to return to the landing page. Navigation to another relevant case study may be added once the set and ordering are defined.

## Open Questions

- Which projects will become case studies?
- How long and detailed should each case study be?
- What visuals or artifacts are available for each project?
- How should case studies with confidential details be presented?
